## Non-negotiable activation rules

- karavi-rule is the policy owner: it loads this matrix and the exact source rule when relevant.
- The delegated skill is the procedure owner: it must be loaded and its evidence recorded.
- A missing or conflicting route becomes decision-required; it is not silently ignored.
- A technical blocker becomes a decision packet; only a hard safety or authorization stop may be blocked.
- A rule with no current trigger remains available but is not activated unnecessarily.
